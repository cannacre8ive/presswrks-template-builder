01 — Set your dimensions

For a 1.25″ or 2″ jar top, choose Custom → Concentrate jar lid and change the diameter. The height stays linked. Presets remain editable too.

02 — Run in Illustrator

Download the .jsx script. In Illustrator, choose File → Scripts → Other Script… and select it. Choose a destination folder; your layered .ait template is created and saved.

03 — Add artwork & verify

CMYK, 300 ppi, ten layers and five spot swatches. Add approved artwork on the named layers, inspect separations, and test fit. Guides and gauge references do not print.

Works offline. No uploads or account required. Dimensions are in decimal inches. Preview is schematic, not actual screen size.
Deli preset uses the source's provisional 2.75″ lid. Tapered body / arc dielines are not generated. Symbol box is a reference, not official symbol artwork or compliance approval.
